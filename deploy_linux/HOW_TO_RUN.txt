HOW TO RUN:
-----------
1. Copy 'autotrader_linux_run.sh' to your Linux computer.
2. Open terminal in that folder and run:

      chmod +x autotrader_linux_run.sh
      ./autotrader_linux_run.sh

That's it! Everything installs, configures, and starts automatically.


OPTIONAL: ACTIVATE PERMANENT NGROK GLOBAL ACCESS (60 Seconds)
-------------------------------------------------------------
If you want to connect your phone globally from outside your home:

1. Create a free account on https://dashboard.ngrok.com
2. Copy your "Authtoken" from the Ngrok dashboard and run:
      ngrok config add-authtoken <YOUR_AUTHTOKEN>

3. Claim your free static domain (e.g., autotrader-xyz.ngrok-free.app) and run:
      ngrok http --domain=<YOUR_DOMAIN> 8000

4. Type your URL (e.g. https://autotrader-xyz.ngrok-free.app) into the
   AutoTrader Phone App Settings and tap "Connect Global Engine".

Done! The connection never changes or expires.


CONTROL COMMANDS:
-----------------
• Live Logs:     journalctl -u autotrader -f
• Restart Bot:   sudo systemctl restart autotrader
• Stop Bot:      sudo systemctl stop autotrader
• Check Status:  sudo systemctl status autotrader
